About

I am Simon Oldebäck, the first of my name.

I am fascinated by bold design that uses color and form in a sophisticated way. Beyond aesthetics, I believe in the power of design to solve problems and connect people.

My goal is to bridge the gap between visual expression and seamless usability. I want to create things that look striking and work flawlessly. Whether a project calls for vibrant energy or sleek minimalism, it never has to feel dull or rigid.
